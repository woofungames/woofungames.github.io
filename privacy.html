English

1. Who we are and scope

Oceanyan Limited operates Woofun Games and Gossip Harbor: Merge & Story (previous project name: Merge Cafe Story). This notice concerns this Android game, its purchase-verification service, and this website. Privacy enquiries: info@woofungames.com.

2. Data and purposes

  • Gameplay on your device: progress, board state, currency, inventory, settings, reward claims and transaction delivery records support saving, restoring local state and preventing duplicate rewards. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove local progress.
  • Purchases: RuStore handles checkout and payment credentials. Our verification service may receive order/invoice identifiers, product, amount, currency, payment status, timestamps and a game-generated player/reference identifier to validate orders and reconcile delivery. Do not send card numbers, passwords or payment verification codes to our support.
  • Optional advertising: when an advertising service is enabled, mediation and ad networks may process device/app identifiers (including an advertising identifier when available), device/OS/app version, language, IP address, network information, approximate region inferred from IP, and ad request, impression, click, completion and reward events. These support delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and rewards. Non-personalized ads can still process technical data.
  • Optional analytics and attribution: MyTracker may process installation/session identifiers, device/app/network information, acquisition-source/referrer information, tutorial and progression events, economy and shop interactions, ad outcomes and purchase outcomes. This helps measure acquisition, retention, progression and monetization. A custom purchase-success event is not itself proof of a verified revenue transaction.
  • Website and support: the hosting provider receives network requests such as IP address, request time and browser information. When you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include.

3. Providers

The integration includes RuStore for purchases, TopOn for ad mediation (including its ADX service), and MyTracker for measurement. Ad-network integrations used or evaluated for this game include Unity Ads, VK/myTarget and BIGO. Inclusion in a build or this list does not mean every network is enabled or receives data on every launch. Other providers must be disclosed before activation.

  • TopOn privacy policy
  • Unity game-player privacy policy
  • MyTracker privacy and security
  • RuStore, VK Ad Network, BIGO Ads (see their current legal/privacy notices).

4. Choices and younger players

The game can be played by different age groups; availability is not parental consent. The forthcoming release is designed to keep optional ads and attribution off for users selecting under 18 or declining to state an age. It asks only for an age band, not a birth date. Adults will have separate, default-off choices for advertising and analytics; declining either must not prevent core offline play. These controls, including withdrawal and SDK shutdown, are still under validation as noted above. In older builds without these controls, contact us for privacy assistance; do not interpret an old consent flag or merely opening the game as a new consent choice. Parents or guardians who believe a child has supplied information should contact us.

5. Sharing, processing locations and legal grounds

Relevant data is processed by payment, hosting, advertising and measurement providers for the purposes described above, and may be disclosed when required by law or to address fraud/security incidents. Providers may process data outside your country; their notices explain their processing and transfer arrangements. We do not claim that all data remains in one country. Where applicable, optional advertising/measurement requires consent; order fulfilment and support relate to providing the requested service, and certain records may be needed for legal obligations. Additional regional consent requirements must be met before enabling the relevant service.

6. Retention and security

Local saves remain until removed or replaced on the device. Server-side order and support records are retained according to the need to verify delivery, resolve disputes, prevent duplicate processing and meet applicable legal obligations; provider-held data is also subject to each provider's retention rules. No fixed retention period is asserted here without a verified configuration. Access should be limited to those who need it, and payment-verification secrets must not be shipped in the app. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Requests and updates

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict data, object to processing, withdraw consent, obtain a portable copy, or complain to a competent authority. Email info@woofungames.com with the game name, request and only the minimum identifier needed to locate your records. We may need proportionate verification. Do not email government identification or payment credentials unless an appropriate secure process has been agreed. Deleting the app does not automatically delete records held by providers. Withdrawal does not invalidate earlier lawful processing. Material changes will be reflected here and, when required, in the app.
